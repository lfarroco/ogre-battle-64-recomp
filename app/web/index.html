Ogre Battle 64: Person of Lordly Caliber — Recompilation (WebAssembly)

Bring-your-own-ROM feasibility build: the recompiled game + N64ModernRuntime running under Emscripten with a null renderer (no graphics yet). Your ROM is read locally by the browser and never leaves this machine.

Controls (keyboard): X=A, Z=B, C=Z, Enter=Start, Q=L, E=R, arrows=D-pad, I/K/J/L=C buttons. Gamepads: slots 1-3. Audio starts after you pick a ROM.
