Ogre Battle 64: Person of Lordly Caliber — Recompilation (WebAssembly)

Bring-your-own-ROM build: the recompiled game + N64ModernRuntime under Emscripten with a WebGL2 renderer prototype (milestone 7) and the null/analyzer path as fallback. Your ROM is read locally by the browser and never leaves this machine.

Controls (keyboard): X=A, Z=B, C=Z, Enter=Start, Q=L, E=R, arrows=D-pad, I/K/J/L=C buttons. Gamepads: slots 1-3. Game audio is muted (the audio microcode is not emulated yet, so the samples screech) — add ?audio to the URL to hear it, and ?log for the full console.
